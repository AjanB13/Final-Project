Our Story

From Garage to Global

Frutex began in a small garage in 1998, when two university friends — a food scientist and a marketing graduate — decided the world deserved better drinks. Armed with little more than a blender, a notebook full of recipes, and an unshakable belief in their vision, they spent nights perfecting what would become their first hit: Golden Eagle.

The name Frutex was chosen carefully. A fusion of "fruit" and "next," it embodied their mission: to take natural flavors and push them into the future. Golden Eagle launched at a single farmers' market stall. Within six months, word of mouth had turned it into a local phenomenon. Within two years, it was on shelves across the country.

Today, Frutex operates in over 40 countries, employs more than 3,000 people, and continues to innovate. Frutti brought tropical joy to millions. Relax redefined what a calming drink could be. But Golden Eagle remains our heart and soul — the one that started it all, and still the one our fans reach for when they need to soar.
